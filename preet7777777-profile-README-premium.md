## Who I Am

I’m a backend-focused developer who likes systems that are practical, scalable, and easy to reason about. My work and learning are centered around Java, cloud-native architecture, serverless workflows, and AI systems that behave well in production.

I enjoy understanding how things break, how to make them observable, and how to design them so they stay reliable under real usage.

---

## Current Focus

- Designing backend systems with `Java`, `REST APIs`, and `event-driven` patterns
- Exploring `AWS Lambda`, `S3`, `API Gateway`, `CloudFront`, and `IAM`
- Learning `LLMOps`, `RAG`, `prompt engineering`, and agent workflows
- Improving `observability`, `debugging`, `reliability`, and production thinking
